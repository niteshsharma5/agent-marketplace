Cedar Ridge Pottery

Cedar Ridge Pottery is a working studio and teaching kiln tucked into the foothills, where we throw functional stoneware for everyday tables and fire it in a wood-burning kiln twice a season. Each mug, bowl, and serving platter is wheel-thrown, trimmed, and glazed by hand, so no two pieces share the exact same speckle or drip. We also run six-week wheel-throwing courses for beginners and open studio hours for returning students who want bench space, clay, and glaze access without signing up for a full term of instruction.

Browse the shelves in person or ask about commissions for restaurants and weddings.
