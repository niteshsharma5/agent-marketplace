Volt Electric Bikes

Volt Electric Bikes designs and services pedal-assist commuter and cargo bikes built for hilly cities and long daily rides. Every model ships with a torque-sensing mid-drive motor, hydraulic disc brakes, integrated lights, and a removable battery that recharges from a standard outlet in about four hours. Our downtown workshop handles tune-ups, warranty claims, and battery diagnostics for any brand, and we offer free thirty-minute test rides along the waterfront path so you can feel the difference before you decide which frame size and gear range fit your commute.

Reserve a demo online or roll into the shop any afternoon this week.
