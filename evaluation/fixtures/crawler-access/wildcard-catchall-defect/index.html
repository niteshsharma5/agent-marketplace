Fjord Kayak Tours

Fjord Kayak Tours runs guided sea-kayaking trips along the western coast, from calm half-day inlet paddles for beginners to multi-day expeditions that camp beneath the cliffs. Our certified guides carry wilderness first-aid credentials, and every trip includes dry suits, marine radios, and a safety briefing tailored to the day's tides and weather. We keep group sizes small so that paddlers of every experience level get real coaching on stroke technique, rescue drills, and reading the water before we ever leave the launch beach.

Book online or stop by the boathouse to check availability for the coming season.
