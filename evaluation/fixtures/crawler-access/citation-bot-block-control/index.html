Meridian Coffee Roasters

Meridian Coffee Roasters is a small-batch roastery based in the harbor district, where we have sourced, cupped, and roasted single-origin beans since 2011. Every lot we buy is traced back to a named farm, and we publish the harvest date, altitude, and processing method for each release so that our customers know exactly what lands in their cup. Our tasting room pours fresh espresso daily and hosts a public cupping every Saturday morning for anyone curious about how flavor develops during the roast.

Visit us to learn about seasonal offerings, brewing gear, and our wholesale program.
