Lumen Dental Studio

Lumen Dental Studio is a family and cosmetic dental practice serving the riverside neighborhood for more than fifteen years. Our team provides routine cleanings, digital X-rays, whitening, aligners, and same-day crowns using low-radiation scanners and a quiet, sedation-friendly treatment room. New patients receive a full oral-health exam and a written plan that explains every recommended procedure, its cost, and the insurance coverage we have already verified on your behalf before any appointment is scheduled, so there are never surprise bills after treatment.
