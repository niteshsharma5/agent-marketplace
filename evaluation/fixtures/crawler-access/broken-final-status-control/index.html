Harbor & Sons Bakery

Harbor & Sons Bakery has milled its own flour and baked naturally leavened bread on Dock Street since 1948, when the first Harbor opened a coal-fired oven two blocks from the fish market. Three generations later we still shape every sourdough loaf by hand, ferment the dough overnight, and bake in the same brick hearth so the crust stays dark and the crumb stays open. Alongside the bread we make morning pastries, seasonal fruit tarts, and custom celebration cakes ordered a week ahead for pickup at the counter.

Stop in early; the popular loaves sell out most mornings before ten.
