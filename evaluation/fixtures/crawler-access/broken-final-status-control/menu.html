Seasonal Menu

Our winter menu leans on the darker, longer-fermented loaves: a rye-caraway boule, a walnut-and-fig sourdough, and the country miche we bake only on weekends. Morning pastries rotate with the fruit that comes in from the valley orchards, so expect quince danishes in November and the first rhubarb galettes as soon as spring arrives at the market stalls.

Whole-grain porridge loaves are available by pre-order for pickup at the Dock Street counter; call ahead a day in advance and we will set one aside for you before the morning rush clears the shelves.
