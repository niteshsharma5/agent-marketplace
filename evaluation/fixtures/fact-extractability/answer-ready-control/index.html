About Our Roasting Process

Where do the beans come from?

We buy single-origin lots directly from farms, chosen for varietal, altitude, and careful hand-picking at harvest.

How do you roast each lot?

Our head roaster builds a dedicated profile per lot, tuning charge temperature, airflow, and development time after first crack.

What happens before shipping?

Every batch is cupped the next morning, then rested for a day so carbon dioxide escapes before we seal and ship it.

  • Sourced directly from farms
  • Profiled and roasted in small batches
  • Cupped, rested, then shipped fresh
