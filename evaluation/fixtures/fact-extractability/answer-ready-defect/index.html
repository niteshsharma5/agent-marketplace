About Our Roasting Process

Our roasting process begins long before any beans reach the drum, because the character of a great cup is decided at origin where farmers make careful choices about varietal selection, altitude, shade cover, harvest timing, and the way ripe cherries are picked and sorted by hand across many weeks of the season. Once green coffee arrives at our warehouse we rest it for a short period so that moisture content settles evenly, and then our head roaster develops a dedicated profile for each lot, adjusting charge temperature, gas application, airflow, and the length of the development phase after first crack so that the sugars caramelise gently without scorching the delicate aromatics that distinguish one farm from another. We taste every batch on a cupping table the following morning, scoring acidity, body, sweetness, and finish against detailed notes, and we will happily reroast a lot that does not meet the standard we promised our subscribers when they trusted us with their weekly ritual. After roasting the coffee rests again for a day or two so carbon dioxide can escape, and only then do we weigh, seal, label, and ship it directly to your door in packaging designed to keep oxygen out and freshness locked in for as long as reasonably possible everywhere we serve.
