Field Notes on Coastal Restoration

We spent the spring season replanting cordgrass across three tidal flats. The volunteers logged growth rates weekly and compared them against last year's plots.

Dune fences went up in April, and by June the windward faces had gathered noticeably more sand than the unfenced control sections nearby.

Next quarter we plan to expand the survey to the eastern inlet, where erosion has cut back the marsh edge by nearly two meters since the last measurement.
