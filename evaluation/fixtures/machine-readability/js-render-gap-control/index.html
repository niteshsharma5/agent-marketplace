Northwind Analytics for Supply-Chain Teams

Northwind Analytics helps logistics and procurement teams turn raw shipment data into decisions they can defend in a quarterly review. We connect to your existing warehouse and order systems, normalize the records, and surface the handful of trends that actually move landed cost.

Most teams we work with start with a single question: where is money leaking between the purchase order and the receiving dock? Our dashboards break that down by lane, carrier, and supplier so you can see whether a spike came from fuel surcharges, detention fees, or a vendor quietly slipping their lead times.

Because the platform is server-rendered, every report is fully readable the moment the page loads. There is no waiting for a client bundle to hydrate before the numbers appear, which keeps the experience fast for analysts working over a VPN and legible for the automated tools that index our public case studies.

Getting started takes about a day. You point us at a read replica or a nightly export, pick the metrics your leadership already tracks, and we backfill twelve months of history so your first dashboard has context instead of a single lonely data point. From there the daily refresh keeps everything current.

What teams measure with us

  • Landed cost per unit, split by lane and carrier.
  • On-time-in-full rates trended against supplier commitments.
  • Detention and demurrage exposure before it hits an invoice.
