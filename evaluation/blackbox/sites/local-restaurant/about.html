Our Story

The Blue Fork opened its doors in 2014, when siblings Marisol and Danny Ortega took over a small storefront on Elm Street that had spent years as a hardware store. Marisol had spent a decade cooking in restaurant kitchens around the region; Danny handled the books and, as he puts it, "kept Marisol from buying a second walk-in fridge she didn't need."

The name comes from a running joke: Marisol's grandmother always set the table with one mismatched blue fork, and no one in the family was ever allowed to throw it out. It's still in a drawer in the kitchen today.

What We Cook

We describe our food as "comfort food with a passport" — classic American plates alongside dishes Marisol picked up while cooking abroad. Everything is made in-house, from the bread down to the pickles. We change the menu with the seasons and lean on a small network of local farms for produce, eggs, and dairy whenever we can.

The Neighborhood

Downtown Millbrook has changed a lot since 2014, and we like to think we've grown up alongside it. We're proud to be a regular stop for the Saturday farmers market crowd, the after-work happy hour regulars, and the families who've been coming in for a decade of birthdays.

Community

We donate a portion of proceeds from our monthly "Community Night" dinner to a rotating local cause, and we host the Millbrook High culinary club for a kitchen tour every spring. If you run a local nonprofit and want to talk about a fundraiser night, reach out through our contact page.
