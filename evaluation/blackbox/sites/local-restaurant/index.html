Welcome to The Blue Fork

Tucked into a corner of downtown Millbrook, The Blue Fork has been serving scratch-made comfort food since 2014. We keep things simple: good ingredients, a warm room, and a menu that changes with the seasons. Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch or settling in for a long dinner with friends, we're glad you're here.
