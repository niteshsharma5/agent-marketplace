About Loomstack

Loomstack is an independent software company based in Madison, Wisconsin. We build project management tools for small teams who want the essentials done well, without a bloated feature list they'll never use.

We started Loomstack in 2019 after spending years running projects on a mix of spreadsheets, sticky notes, and shared inboxes. Today the team is eleven people, and Loomstack is used by roughly 3,400 teams across design studios, construction firms, and marketing agencies. We remain self-funded and have never taken outside investment, which lets us build for our customers instead of a fundraising deadline.

How we work

We ship small improvements every few weeks rather than large redesigns once a year, and we read every support email ourselves — there is no outsourced support queue. If a feature request comes up often enough, you'll usually see it shipped within a couple of months.

Contact us

Questions about a plan, a feature, or anything else? Reach out and a real person on our small team will get back to you, usually within one business day.
