Everything a small team needs, nothing it doesn't

Loomstack is built for teams of 3 to 50 people who have outgrown a shared spreadsheet but don't want the overhead of enterprise software. Every plan includes task boards, a timeline view, and shared team docs.

Task boards

Organize work into columns your team already understands: To Do, In Progress, and Done. Drag cards between them as work moves forward.

Timeline view

See every task laid out against the calendar so you can spot scheduling conflicts before they become deadline problems.

Shared docs

Keep meeting notes, briefs, and specs next to the tasks they relate to, instead of scattered across email and chat.

Simple permissions

Invite clients or contractors as guests with view-only access, while your core team keeps full edit rights.
