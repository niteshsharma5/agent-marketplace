Pricing questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full features and no credit card required to start.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings at any time, and billing is prorated for the current cycle.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Paying annually gives you two months free compared to the monthly price on any plan.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your workspace stays read-only and exportable for 30 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted.
