Harmon County Open Records Act — Chapter 12

12.101 — Declaration of Policy

It is the public policy of Harmon County that all persons are entitled to full and complete information regarding the affairs of government and the official acts of those who represent them as public officials and employees, consistent with the terms of this chapter. The provisions of this chapter shall be liberally construed to implement this policy, and the exceptions enumerated in section 12.107 shall be narrowly construed to effectuate this legislative intent.

12.102 — Definitions

As used in this chapter: (a) "Public record" means any document, paper, letter, map, book, tape, photograph, microfilm, electronic data processing record, or other documentary material, regardless of physical form or characteristics, made or received pursuant to law or ordinance or in connection with the transaction of official business by any county agency. (b) "County agency" means any office, department, board, commission, or other administrative unit of Harmon County government, however denominated. (c) "Custodian" means the county officer or employee having personal custody and control of the public record in question.

12.103 — Right of Access

Every person who is given the custody of a public record shall permit any person desiring to inspect the same to inspect it at reasonable times without charge, and to obtain a copy of the record upon payment of a fee not to exceed the actual cost of duplication, subject to the exceptions enumerated in section 12.107. Requests need not be in writing unless the custodian reasonably requires a written request in order to verify due execution or in order to prepare the requested record.

12.104 — Time for Response

A custodian who receives a request for a public record shall, within a reasonable period of time, permit inspection or copying, or certify that the record is exempt, or certify that the record does not exist. If the request is voluminous or requires legal review to determine applicability of an exemption under section 12.107, the custodian may extend the response period, provided that the requester is notified in writing of the reason for the extension and the anticipated date of response.

12.105 — Fees

A custodian may charge a fee for copies of public records, provided that the fee does not exceed the actual cost of duplication. No fee shall be charged for the inspection of records where no copy is made. Certified copies may be subject to an additional statutory certification fee as set by the county fee schedule then in effect.

12.106 — Format of Records

If a public record exists in an electronic format, the custodian shall, upon request, provide a copy of the record in that electronic format if it is practicable to do so, unless the requester agrees to accept the record in a different format.

12.107 — Exemptions

The following records, and portions of records, are exempt from disclosure under this chapter: (a) records the disclosure of which is prohibited by federal or state law; (b) records containing personal identifying information of a minor; (c) active criminal investigative records; (d) attorney work product and records protected by attorney-client privilege; (e) personnel records to the extent disclosure would constitute an unwarranted invasion of personal privacy; (f) security plans and vulnerability assessments for county infrastructure. A custodian asserting an exemption under this section bears the burden of establishing that the exemption applies and shall disclose any reasonably segregable non-exempt portion of a record after redacting the portion claimed to be exempt.

12.108 — Enforcement

Any person denied the right to inspect or copy a public record may bring an action in the county circuit court to compel disclosure. If the court finds that the county agency acted in bad faith in denying access, the court may award reasonable attorney fees and costs to the prevailing requester.
