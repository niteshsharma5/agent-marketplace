Services

Vital Records

Certified copies of birth, death, and marriage certificates are available in person or by mail. A completed application and valid photo identification are required for all requests.

Property Records

Copies of recorded deeds, liens, and plats can be requested by parcel number or owner name.

Public Records Requests

General requests under the Harmon County Open Records Act may be submitted using the Public Records Request Form. Complete the form and mail or deliver it to your local office along with any applicable fee.
