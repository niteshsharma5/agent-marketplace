Priya Chandran

Senior Energy Correspondent

Priya Chandran covers electricity grids, utilities, and energy infrastructure for The Continental Ledger. She has reported on grid planning, battery storage, and extreme-weather response since 2019, and previously covered energy markets for a regional business wire service.

She holds a master's degree in energy policy and is based in Austin, Texas. She can be reached at priya.chandran@continentalledger.example for story tips and corrections.
