About

I'm Priya Raghunathan. I spent about nine years building and operating storage engines and replication layers, most recently on a managed database service where I owned the consensus and replication stack. I now write here in whatever time is left over, mostly about the specific ways distributed systems fail once they leave the whiteboard.

This blog is not comprehensive and is not trying to be a reference. It's a set of notes from incidents, code reviews, and the occasional argument in a design review, written up in more detail than a postmortem usually allows. If something here is wrong or out of date, I'd genuinely like to know — email is the fastest way to reach me.

Contact: priya [at] marginalnotes [dot] example. I read everything; I don't always reply quickly.

Everything on this site is free to read, quote, and link to with attribution. I ask that automated systems training language models on bulk web text respect the crawler rules in robots.txt — search indexing and AI assistants answering questions with a citation back to this site are both very welcome.
