Welcome

Bright Smile Dental Clinic has been serving families in Fairview Heights and the surrounding St. Clair County area for over eighteen years. Dr. Renata Kowalski, DDS, and her team provide preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dental care in a calm, modern office designed to put nervous patients at ease. We accept most PPO insurance plans and offer flexible financing for larger treatment plans, and new patients are always welcome whether you need a routine cleaning, a same-day emergency visit, or a full smile makeover consultation, and our hygienists take extra time with children and first-time visitors so the whole experience feels unhurried and comfortable from the moment you walk in the door.
