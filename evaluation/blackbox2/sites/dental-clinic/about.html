About Bright Smile Dental Clinic

Our Story

Dr. Renata Kowalski, DDS, opened Bright Smile Dental Clinic in 2008 after completing her residency at Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. She and her husband chose Fairview Heights because it was close to family, and the practice has grown from a single treatment room to a five-chair office with three hygienists and a dedicated pediatric-friendly waiting area. Dr. Kowalski still personally performs new-patient exams and complex restorative work while her associate dentist, Dr. Michael Ferris, DMD, handles a portion of the general and cosmetic caseload, and both dentists complete continuing education hours well beyond the state requirement each year so that the office can keep offering current techniques like same-day crowns and digital impression scanning rather than the older physical mold process many patients remember from childhood visits to other clinics.
