About Aurelie & Co.

Founded in 2018 in Porto, Portugal, Aurelie & Co. makes wool and cashmere outerwear meant to last for years of wear, not one season.

Where it's made

Every coat and scarf is cut and finished in our own workshop in Porto. We work with a small group of mills in Portugal and Italy who can tell us exactly which farm each batch of wool came from.

Materials

We use only merino wool and cashmere, chosen for how they wear over time rather than trend cycles. Every fabric is audited annually for animal welfare and land use standards before we place an order.

Get in touch

Questions about an order or a wholesale inquiry? Email us at support@aurelieandco.example and we'll reply within one business day.
