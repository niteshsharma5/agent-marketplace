Department of Computer Science

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science at Northfield State University. The department was founded in 1974 and has grown from a small program housed within the Mathematics division into one of the largest undergraduate and graduate computing programs in the region, offering coursework and research opportunities across the full breadth of the discipline including but not limited to algorithms, artificial intelligence, computer systems, databases, human-computer interaction, networking, programming languages, software engineering, and theoretical computer science, and our faculty regularly publish in top venues and collaborate with industry partners on funded research projects that give undergraduate and graduate students alike the opportunity to gain hands-on experience well before they graduate and enter the workforce or pursue further study at the doctoral level, and we take pride in maintaining small class sizes and an open-door policy among our faculty so that every student, regardless of background or prior preparation, has direct access to mentorship throughout their time in the program.

The department is pleased to announce that Professor Halvorsen's group received a three-year grant to study distributed systems reliability, that the annual undergraduate research symposium will be held in the spring semester with abstract submissions accepted from all currently enrolled majors, that the graduate admissions committee has finished its review of applications for the coming academic year and decisions will be released on a rolling basis over the following weeks, and that the department computer labs in the Ellery Hall basement have been upgraded with new workstations for the coming term, all of which reflects the continued growth and investment the university is making in the computing program as demand for our majors and courses continues to increase year over year across nearly every concentration we offer.

Degree Programs

We offer a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science for students pairing the major with a second field of study, a Master of Science in Computer Science with thesis and non-thesis tracks, and a Ph.D. in Computer Science for students pursuing research careers in academia or industry; further details on each track, including required coursework, elective options, and typical time to completion, are available from academic advisors during the scheduled advising periods each semester or by appointment throughout the year.
