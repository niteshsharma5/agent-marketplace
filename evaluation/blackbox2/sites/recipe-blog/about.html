Why I Started Grandma's Kitchen

Hi, I'm Marge Callahan. Grandma's Kitchen started as a private recipe binder I kept for my own kids, so that the recipes my mother and grandmother cooked wouldn't get lost the way so many family recipes do. A few years in, I started sharing them here so other families could give them a try too.

Every recipe on this site is tested in my own kitchen, usually more than once, before it goes up. If a recipe didn't turn out right the first three times I made it, it doesn't get published — simple as that.

Thanks for stopping by, and I hope something here becomes a regular in your kitchen too.
