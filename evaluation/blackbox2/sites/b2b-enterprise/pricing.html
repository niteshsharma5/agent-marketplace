Pricing questions

Is there a setup fee?

Team and Business plans have no setup fee. Enterprise plans include a one-time onboarding engagement scoped with your solutions engineer, priced separately from the subscription.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What counts as an active user?

An active user is anyone who submits, approves, or resolves at least one workflow item in a billing month. Occasional approvers who only click approve from email still count, but system accounts and API integrations do not.
