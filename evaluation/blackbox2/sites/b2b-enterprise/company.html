Company

Built by people who spent years cleaning up approval chains by hand

Meridian Ops was founded in 2016 by former finance-operations and platform engineers who were tired of watching audits fall apart over missing sign-offs.

Our story

Meridian Ops started inside a mid-size insurer's finance team, where founders Priya Natarajan and Tom Hargrove were manually reconciling approval spreadsheets before every audit cycle. After building an internal tool to automate it, they spun the idea out as a standalone company in 2016.

Today, more than 240 enterprises in financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing run their approval and reconciliation workflows on Meridian Ops. The company is privately held and headquartered in San Francisco, with a remote engineering team across North America and Europe.

Security & compliance

Meridian Ops handles financial approval and reconciliation data, so security is built into the platform rather than added on top.

SOC 2 Type II

Independently audited annually. The current report is available under NDA — request it through your account team or via the contact form below.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest with AES-256. Enterprise plans support customer-managed encryption keys.

Data residency

Customers can choose US or EU hosting regions at signup. Data does not move between regions without an explicit migration request.

Access reviews

Enterprise plans include an annual joint access review with your security team, covering roles, permissions, and integration scopes.
