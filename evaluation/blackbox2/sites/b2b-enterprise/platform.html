Platform overview

One workflow engine for approvals, exceptions, and audit trails

Meridian Ops sits between the systems your teams already use and gives every request, exception, and sign-off a single, traceable home.

Approval orchestration

Design multi-step approval chains with conditional routing based on amount, department, vendor risk, or any field from a connected system. Delegate approvals during time off without losing the audit trail, and set automatic escalation when a request sits too long.

Approvers act from email, Slack, Teams, or the Meridian Ops inbox — no separate login required for occasional approvers.

Exception reconciliation

Meridian Ops compares records across connected systems and surfaces mismatches automatically, before they turn into audit findings.

Automatic matching

Match invoices, purchase orders, and receipts across systems using configurable rules, with tolerance thresholds you control.

Owner routing

Unmatched or out-of-tolerance items are routed to the correct owner automatically, based on department, vendor, or cost center.

Resolution tracking

Every exception carries a full resolution history, so recurring issues are easy to spot and fix at the source.

Connects to the systems you already run

Native connectors keep data in sync without middleware, and a documented REST API covers everything else.

Need a connector we don't list yet? Our solutions team scopes custom connectors as part of enterprise onboarding — get in touch.

Built for enterprise IT and security teams

Role-based access control

Permissions apply down to the individual workflow step, so a reviewer in one department can't see requests from another.

SSO & SCIM

Provision and deprovision users automatically through your identity provider, with SAML-based single sign-on for every login.

Immutable audit log

Every state change is recorded with a user, timestamp, and reason, and exportable in CSV or via API for your GRC tooling.
