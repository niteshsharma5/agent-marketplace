Frequently asked questions

How long does implementation take?

Most customers complete their first live workflow within 2 to 4 weeks of kickoff, using our guided onboarding and pre-built connector templates. Full multi-department rollouts typically take 8 to 12 weeks.

Does Meridian Ops integrate with our existing ERP?

Meridian Ops ships native connectors for the major ERP, HRIS, and ITSM platforms used by enterprise operations teams, and a documented REST API for anything not covered out of the box. See the integrations section for details.

Is Meridian Ops compliant with SOC 2 and similar standards?

Yes. Meridian Ops is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and supports customer-managed retention policies. Details are on the company page.

Can I try Meridian Ops before purchasing?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full platform access and no credit card required. See pricing for plan details.
